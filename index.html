          Good questions

          What is copy trading?

          Copy trading lets your HFM account automatically mirror the trades I place on my strategy. You stay in control — you choose how much to allocate and can stop copying at any time. Past results never guarantee future results.

          Do I need experience to start?

          No. Start with the free weekly setup and the beginner lessons. If you'd rather not trade manually yet, copy trading lets you take part while you learn.

          Which broker do you use?

          I trade with HFM. You can open an account through the "Open account" button on this page. Funding options, including mobile money, depend on what HFM offers in your country.

          Are the setups financial advice?

          No. Everything on Forex Nerds is research and education. Always do your own analysis, use a stop loss and only risk money you can afford to lose.
